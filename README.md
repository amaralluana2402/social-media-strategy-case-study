# Social Media Strategy — Case Study

A strategic Social Media project focused on audience analysis, content planning, creative direction and performance.

## Project Overview

This case study was created to demonstrate my strategic process in Social Media, from research and positioning to content planning and performance analysis.

The goal is to build a complete Social Media strategy that connects brand objectives, audience behavior and content execution.

## Objectives

- Understand the brand and its communication challenges
- Identify the target audience
- Analyze competitors and market positioning
- Define strategic content pillars
- Create an editorial direction
- Develop content and Reels ideas
- Define KPIs to measure performance

## Strategic Process

### 1. Brand Analysis

Understanding the brand, its positioning, communication style and main goals.

### 2. Target Audience

Mapping audience characteristics, interests, behaviors and content preferences.

### 3. Competitor Analysis

Analyzing how similar brands communicate, what content formats they use and where opportunities exist.

### 4. Content Pillars

Defining the main themes that will guide the brand's Social Media presence.

### 5. Editorial Planning

Creating a structured content plan with formats, frequency and objectives.

### 6. Content Creation

Developing ideas for:

- Instagram posts
- Carousels
- Reels
- Stories
- Campaign content
- Community engagement

### 7. Performance Strategy

Defining the main KPIs to monitor:

- Reach
- Engagement
- Saves
- Shares
- Follower growth
- Profile visits
- Link clicks

## Deliverables

This case study will include:

- Brand diagnosis
- Audience analysis
- Competitor analysis
- Content pillars
- Editorial calendar
- Reels ideas
- Copywriting examples
- Visual content examples
- KPI framework
- Final strategic recommendations
